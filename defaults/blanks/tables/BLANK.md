Gated by `table-lookups-mode` in OPENCUES.md (off by default): while off this
blank is not registered at all, and the `_` route asks no table question.

Implementation: built-in `TablesBlank` in `@opencues/runtime`
(`packages/opencues-runtime/src/blanks/tables.ts`). One blank, eight
OFFLINE tables (`tables-data.ts`) and a calculator library
(`blanks/calc/`: dates & durations, timezones, number formatting & number
theory; more families follow): the lookups people type with a `_` that a
table or a pure function answers exactly. No network, no model, no
hallucination — the answer is data or arithmetic. Country facts keep their
own blank (`countries`).

- `meeting at 3pm london for new york tokyo _` → `London 15:00 · New York 10:00 · Tokyo 23:00`
- `1234567 in words _` → `one million, two hundred and thirty-four thousand, five hundred and sixty-seven`
- `255 in hex _` → `0xff (binary 11111111, octal 377)`
- `0.375 as a fraction _` → `3/8`
- `prime factors of 360 _` → `2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 5 (2³ · 3² · 5)`
- `stats of 3 5 8 13 _` → `mean 7.25 · median 6.5 · sd 4.35 · min 3 · max 13 · n 4`
- `5 choose 2 _` → `10 combinations (20 permutations)`

With `decisions-provider` on, a plain phrasing (`what's the postgres port _`,
`how many feet in a mile _`) can reach the same tables through the `_`
route's `table` verdict (core's `decisions/data-policy.ts`): the verdict names
the table, the runtime resolves the argument under a closed grammar and
runs this blank. Without a decision package the shapes above are the only
way in. ReadOnly: cycling is a no-op.

# In Ram

In Ram (Having the full state of a product or system in your head allowing for efficient manipulation, invention and discovery) Against full Ai adoption

When using agentic tools one of the side-effects I make an effort to avoid is the project not being 'In Ram'.

In Ram being the phenomena where when working on something you particularly coding projects you are able to efficient explore the codebase, debug issues, review a system's architecture, plan new features, identify implications and other aspects all without looking at or touching the codebase, because you have a mirror representation of the system 'In Ram'.

It was one of the most rewarding aspects about building software. You would eventually arrive at a point where the system is fully grokked and you achieve subconscious insights, e.g. during walks, whilst you're sleeping or in the shower.

One of the potential pitfalls with LLM based systems is that individuals can allow for a varying degrees of drift between the codebase and their 'In Ram' representation of the system - worst yet some individuals omit maintaining a representation altogether.

Many individuals would argue for vibe coding, but I utilise the tactic myself but I am always over-eager to be the system architect - to have an accurate representation of the system 'In Ram'.

This allows for faster iteration and cross-domain pollination. One of the greatest benefits of building with agentic tools is that when you have an insight about a new aspect you might integrate into a system, building the integration is no longer the bottle neck. Determine the implications, potential contribution towards technical debt, relevance, integrate-ability are now bigger burdens. So having a mental model of the system in mind allows for exploration and experimentation without wasting tokens.

Additionally an unproven side-effect of holding things 'In Ram' is you might increase how much ram you have. As I said, unproven but it has worked for me - as I have worked on more and more complex projects and cross-pollinated across various industries and domains I have found it easier to hold greater degrees of complexities or numbers of mechanics 'In Ram'.

Avoid the temptation to overly rely on an LLM to maintain the knowledge of a system. Even in the case where you're only noting 50% of a system's complexity, aspire to maintain 'In Ram' twice or thrice as many systems. I am certain you'll discover opportunities for cross pollination.

# What is Design

The term 'design' is somewhat hard to define. It has many embodiments and interpretations, I approach the definition from the perspective of what is a 'designer'. There are many pre-fixes which are put in front of the term designer and within those disciplines there are individuals which are more or less adept.
At what skill level is an individual allowed to adorn the title of 'X designer', I believe this is the misnomer… There is no skill level threshold which you must meet to be able to claim to be an 'X designer', because I hold the belief that.

> To design is to decide

We all make decisions, in work, in various disciplines and in our lives. Would I consider my life to be 'designed', yes - I intentionally make decisions (some better than others) which culminate in a 'designed' life.

Attempting to follow that analogy I believe it is not a case of one person being a designer and another being not a designer, but a case of someone being more negligent of the 'decisions' they make when pursuing an endeavour. This negligence when making decisions partially contributes to the variance in output.

Bringing it back to the original question, I believe design is to decide. We are all continuously making decisions and thus designing. To be better "designer's" we need to educate ourselves about the implications of our decisions and just generally care!

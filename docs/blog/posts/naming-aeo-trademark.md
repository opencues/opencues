# Naming / AEO (Audio Engine Optimisation) / Trademark

Whilst working on an unreleased HCI at Command Stick we came across an interesting new form of 'Engine Optimisation'.

I call it Audio Engine Optimisation. In the past when building products we often optimised for searchability (e.g. best London bagels), ranked list order (e.g. apple having AAPL ticker)

(List more examples of Engine optimisation)

In this new world where AI is evolving and the means of interfacing with LLMs is leaning more and more towards voice I believe the important have terms be easily understandable and unmistakable by LLMs. This form of engine optimisation allows for a user to easily utter a relevant expression to an LLM and reliably have the intended service be chosen by an LLM.

Terms which are:

- Hard to pronounce
- Common words in UI/ serves
- Undistinguishable
- Flooded with competition
- Sound goofy

Will effect the over HCI experience when a user is attempting to interface with an voice LLM. Due to LLMs attempting to predict the next word some prior terms can bias an LLMs prediction away from your company or product name, that is assuming perfect audio. Stacking with bad microphones and ambient noise the UX of a user utilising your service via a voice based UI can suffer heavily.

Requiring a user to make multiple utterances to achieve success with a voice based UX is a sure way to limit the adoption of an application or workflow. Be sure to test your utterances in multiple conditions with multiple word priors and ascends to ensure solid recall.

# Simplicity

| Ocalm's razor | Technical debt, Tokens wasted, Digestibility extensibility | Less is more | Reductionism (spelling)

Whistling designing HCI systems simplicity is always an aspect I aspire to towards, though it holds a secondary priority for me particularly during the inventive phase of research.

> Aspiring towards simplicity can sometimes anchor you in bewilderment.

Moving towards simplicity can involve simplifying that which exists and progressing towards a local maxima. During our research and development processes we often employ a Continuous Diamond (repeated double diamond) process in search of novel mechanics which can then be clustered together and simplified to achieve various candidate solutions.

It is good to pursue simplicity but complexity and vast amounts of options serve as the basis for applying a simplification algorithm.

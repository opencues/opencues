# The Value of Community

As the moderator of r/ClaudeAI over the last 10 months or so I have learned endless amounts from the community. Not just from that which is posted, but that which does not make it through. Seeing how a community can galvanise around a product and can be the pulse/ reflection of my own product experience has been enlightening.

In combination with the endless swath of OpenSource projects which are birthed from the community on a daily basis. It makes me remember why I first pivoted from being a designer to being more in the technical domain.

> Those coders over there, they all work together to reach new heights. (Standing on the shoulders of giants)

It is a beautiful thing to watch a community develop and grow awesome projects which then blossom and develop their own communities around them.

My hope with this project is that I can pour my skillset into working with the communities online to explore and build the future of AI interaction.

This is one of the main reasons we are also working on an OpenStandard for how aspects of an Inline Prompting and Inline Agent could work.

I am looking forward to getting guidance and direction from not just skilled engineers and skilled designers but normal folk who want AI interaction to be better!

Tier-2 on top of offset bucketing: setup measures NOTHING — a word's geometry (one getClientRects() + cumulative measureText) is built lazily on the first tick that re-rolls it, and words in off-screen paragraphs (IntersectionObserver, 100px margin) stop re-rolling once the observer reports them out, so per-tick cost tracks the viewport, not the document. Paragraphs default to visible so a starved observer degrades to "no culling", never "no animation". Tier-1: cadence comes from setTimeout(70), but each tick's mutations are offered to the next real frame via requestAnimationFrame (24ms fallback runs unaligned when frames are starved — headless, prerender), and hidden tabs skip the work entirely. Scroll note: a word that scrolls out mid-run keeps its frozen scramble until it scrolls back in and re-rolls; X-offsets survive vertical scroll because only intra-word deltas are ever used.

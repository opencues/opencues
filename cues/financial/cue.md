When the highlighted word is a financial term, suggest alternatives
that preserve financial meaning. Distinguish between:
- "equity" (ownership stake) vs "stock" (traded instrument) vs "shares"
- "yield" (return on investment) vs "interest" vs "return"
- "leverage" (borrowed capital) vs "debt" vs "margin"
- "hedge" (risk mitigation) vs "insurance" vs "protection"
- "liquidity" (ease of conversion) vs "cash flow" vs "solvency"

Prefer standard financial terminology over informal language.
Do not conflate accounting terms with casual usage (e.g., "depreciation"
in finance vs general "decline").

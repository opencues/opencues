When suggesting alternatives for clinical terms, prefer ICD-10 standard
terminology. Group related terms by clinical relationship:
- "diagnosis" links to "differential diagnosis", "clinical impression"
- "prognosis" links to "outcome", "disease course"
- "contraindication" links to "precaution", "adverse interaction"

Never suggest informal patient-facing language as alternatives for
clinical terms. Maintain HIPAA-appropriate terminology throughout.
